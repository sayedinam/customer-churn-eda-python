# 📉 Customer Churn & Retention Analysis (Python EDA)

## 📌 Business Problem
Customer attrition directly impacts monthly recurring revenue. The objective of this exploratory data analysis (EDA) project is to analyze customer demographic, contract, and billing data to identify key factors leading to customer churn and provide data-backed retention strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- **Language:** Python
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Focus:** Data Cleaning, Outlier Detection, Exploratory Data Analysis, Feature Correlation

---

## 🔍 Key Insights Discovered
1. **Contract Type Impact:** Customers on Month-to-Month contracts have a churn rate exceeding 40%, whereas Two-Year contract churn is under 5%.
2. **Payment Methods:** Customers utilizing Electronic Checks show significantly higher churn compared to credit card auto-pay users.
3. **Tenure Dynamics:** The highest customer attrition occurs within the first 6 months of onboarding.
4. **Pricing Sensitivity:** High monthly charges directly correlate with customer drop-off in early tenures.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
- **Onboarding Incentives:** Introduce introductory milestone discounts for the first 90 days to increase customer stickiness.
- **Contract Transition Campaigns:** Offer targeted incentives for month-to-month users to transition into annual plans.
- **Automated Billing Promotions:** Encourage transitions to credit card auto-pay with a one-time bill credit.
